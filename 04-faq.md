# 4. FAQ

**Q1. What happens if an item is out of stock?**
The sale is blocked and the item appears on the low-stock report.

**Q2. Who can change the reorder level?**
Only the Store Manager.

**Q3. How is the order quantity decided?**
`max_stock - current_quantity`, so stock is refilled up to its maximum.

**Q4. Can a purchase order be edited after approval?**
No. It must be cancelled and a new one raised.
