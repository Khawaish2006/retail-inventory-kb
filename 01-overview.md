# 1. System Overview

**Purpose:** Track every product in a retail store so that the right items are always in stock
without over-ordering.

## Users
- **Store Manager** – sets reorder levels, approves purchase orders, views reports.
- **Cashier** – records sales at the billing counter.
- **Supplier** – receives purchase orders and delivers stock.

## Modules
1. Product Catalogue – product ID, name, category, price.
2. Stock Management – current quantity and reorder level of each product.
3. Sales / Billing – reduces stock when an item is sold.
4. Purchase Orders – raised to suppliers when stock is low.
5. Reports – low-stock, daily sales and stock valuation.
